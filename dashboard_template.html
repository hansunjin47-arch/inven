MOPSO-AMS vs. PCN Family

Han et al. (2023)'s adaptive multi-swarm MOPSO (MOPSO-AMS) compared against plain MOPSO and the PCN family (Original PCN, PCN with buffer init., Guided PCN with softmax objective selection) across all six demand settings. 10 runs per method per setting; CMODE omitted from this comparison. MOPSO-AMS uses the same 200-particle x 100-iteration evaluation budget as MOPSO.
